Composing Digital Media Portfolio

Introduction to New Media


My Digital Media Narrative

This project described my past experiences with technologies and coding. It demonstrates how technology has influenced my life and how it affects the work I do now.

Webtext Experiment

This project was my experiment with using text coding to create simple animations. I wanted to combine my interest in art and animation with coding.

Multi-Page Webtext Experiment

I made this project to combine my love of storytelling with coding. I was able to experiment with creating a multi-paged project in the form of a "choose your own adventure" story.

Course Goals


1. Consider technology’s role in acts of making

The multi-page assignment made me think the most about how I could use technology for creation. I have always enjoyed writing narratives and storytelling but never thought about using technology in that process. By creating a narrative for my multi-page assignment, I was able to see how technology can enhance a story. The ability to create links between pages made a fast and easy way to make decisions in a "choose-your-own-adventure" type of narrative. Technology makes this process far more efficient than simple pen and paper can. I was allowed more creative freedom by changing the fonts and adding pictures, something that would be far more difficult without technology. That project showed me the value in including technology in acts of making and how technology can enhance the final product.

2. Embrace experimentation

I didn't have much experience with coding so every project was an experiment for me. I had to constantly try new things and use trial and error to create my projects. I did the most experimentation in my web text experiment, playing around with text boxes and "animations" I could include for them. To be honest, a lot of what I achieved in that project was done by accident while I was playing around with settings and code I could put in. I had very little knowledge about HTML and coding, so I felt encouraged to experiment to see what could be done using these tools. This project was a good place for it because the single-page format was very doable and not daunting. It gave me the courage to experiment instead of just trying to submit an assignment.

3.Engage with others in a workshop environment

Because I wasn't very familiar with code, the workshop environment was very important. My digital media narrative especially allowed me to interact with my classmates and learn from them how I could improve my projects and fix problems I was having. I had essentially no coding experience when I first went into this project, so I came to workshop with many problems. I couldn't even get my website published through GitHub. Engaging with others is what ended up being my saving grace. I basically handed Sarah my laptop with the request, "please just fix it". She was very kind, extremely helpful, and did it fact, "fix it". She showed me how to properly format my files and upload them through GitHub. Even from the first project, I learned that many of my classmates had skills with coding that I didn't possess. I knew from then on that it was incredibly crucial that I took the advice of my classmates and used their projects to better my own.

4. Practice revision within and across media forms

Revision was a big part of my process because often, my projects did not fully work the first time. I learned quickly how difficult it was to make my work in Visual Studio Code compatible with GitHub on the first try. My Digital Media Narrative was the biggest example of this. I spent all of workshop that class editing the code and my files to try and make my website open. I was lucky I had that time to work through my mistakes and figure out that the main page needed to be named "index", regardless of what I put into it. My various attempts during this project taught me the skills I used in future projects. By my multi-page narrative, I was able to publish my website on my first try. I learned revising was incredibly important for learning from mistakes and making that best project I can.

5. Learn to present your work

I presented two out of three of my new media projects. The first time, it was my Media Narrative, and I wasn't sure exactly what I was supposed to be presenting. I explained the words on my screen, but not as much of my actual process. It was the first presentation I had done where I was showing a project I made rather than explaining information to a group. From watching other presentations, I gathered that my presentation was much more about my process and the purpose of my project rather than what I actually made. I was able to show this much better in my second presentation of my Webtext Media Experiment. It was here that I described how and why I did things rather than just what I did. It gave me knew insight into what presenting was actually for and how to accomplish that style of presenting.

Sound and Image


Sound Experiment

This sound piece was my first time experimenting with sound. I wanted to combine intersting sounds I found around Oakland on the day that I filmed.

Exploratory Podcast

I enjoyed trying out the podcast format in my Exploratory podcast. I wanted to explore a less serious concept and the real implications of it.

Photo Collage

I found this quote by Barthes to be interesting and I wanted to explore the natural pairs things come in. InDesign was not the easiest program to use so I had to experiment to learn how to utilize it. The website is the best way I found to make a PDF openable.

Photo Essay

In my photo essay, I wanted to explore how art can come in different forms by exploring the graffiti I found around Oakland. I furthered this concept with the music and poem I chose.

Course Goals

1. Consider technology’s role in acts of making

The Photo Essay was particularly interesting to me as a new medium of creating. I hadn't considered much the impact that still images could have when combined with video. Using Premiere Pro was a completely new experience for me and for a bit I struggled. However, as I used it more, I was able to see the potential it had as a great device for creating. There was a lot you could adjust with formatting and layering, and it was very helpful with making the project exactly as you want it. I struggled with doing everything I wanted with the complication of the program, but it made me want to keep learning to use its full potential.


2. Embrace experimentation

I felt I was able to embrace experimentation with all of the sound and image projects. Though it is on the nose, the Sound Experiment was very experimental for me. I felt the most ability to explore and simply do whatever I wanted to with the project. I played around a lot with different ways of recording. I tried getting closer and farther away. I tried ways to avoid extra environmental noises and then later I tried different ways of cutting the recordings to change the way it came together. Because the limitations were so broad, I felt the most room to experiment with and try out new ideas.


3.Engage with others in a workshop environment

I found the most value in engaging with my classmates in the Exploratory Podcast. I enjoyed the time we had that class period to discuss with my peers their projects and how they found success. I learned the importance of different effects and the benefit of playing direct clips to back up your discussion. I received a lot of good feedback on my podcast, like the way to format each portion of my podcast and new ways to introduce sources to your podcast. The longer format of the podcast created a lot of content to workshop and broadened the amount of feedback I could receive.


4. Practice revision within and across media forms

I practiced the most revision with my Photo Collage. I learned when I entered class that day that I had not really understood the format I was trying to emulate. This forced me to go back into my project and think about more of what I needed to do. I had to consider how to take the design and purpose of my current project and change it to better fit the assignment. As I revised, the point of the assignment started to make more sense to me, and I saw the importance of this specific structure. Media is very important when you are conveying a message, and different formats can change your message. I only realized this idea through my revision.


5. Learn to present your work

The only project I presented in this section was my sound experiment. This was sort of a vulnerable presentation for me because the guidelines of the project were so open. I was worried that I misunderstood the assignment, or that my classmates wouldn't understand what I was trying to convey. I ended up receiving a lot of support that I was grateful for. Presenting a project that is open to interpretation like that is a unique experience because purpose and though-process is very important. It is different than just explaining how you did something or the content that is in it.
